The research

Where AI work breaks down, and what the numbers say

By Lakshmi Peri, founder of Naanya · Updated October 2026

Nine places AI initiatives go wrong, from the first idea to months after launch. Each one is backed by named, dated research, and paired with how Naanya closes the gap.

Every number links to its source."As reported by" means we confirmed the figure through news coverage of the original report.

01 · Intake

Why does AI work start without anyone seeing it?

78%

of AI users bring their own AI tools to work.Microsoft & LinkedIn, 2024 Work Trend Index · source

35%

of leaders at companies with 1,000+ employees say their AI tools go through proper approval.Zapier survey of 550 US leaders, Dec 2025 (vendor survey) · source

How Naanya closes it

One front door for every AI idea, and a single list of every tool in use, so new work has an easy path in instead of a hidden one.

02 · Business case

Why are so many AI projects abandoned after the pilot?

30%

of generative AI projects predicted to be abandoned after proof of concept by the end of 2025, partly due to unclear business value.Gartner, Jul 2024, as reported by Intelligent CIO · source

46%

of AI proofs of concept were scrapped before reaching production, on average.S&P Global Market Intelligence, as reported by CIO Dive, Mar 2025 · source

How Naanya closes it

Every idea is scored the same way before anything is built. It has to solve a frequent, painful problem to earn a trial.

03 · Baseline

Why can't companies prove their AI projects are working?

95%

of enterprise generative AI pilots showed no rapid revenue gain.MIT NANDA, The GenAI Divide, as reported by Fortune, Aug 2025 · source

39%

of organizations report any profit impact from AI, though 88% use it.McKinsey State of AI 2025, as reported by Silicon Canals · source

How Naanya closes it

No trial starts without today's cost measured and a clear target. At day 90 the result is checked against that number.

04 · Ownership

Who owns AI initiatives, and why does it matter?

88%

of CIOs say AI uptake is outpacing oversight, with no agreed model for who owns it.Thoughtworks survey of 3,200 CIOs, as reported by CIO Dive, Oct 2026 · source

94%

of US CIOs expect to be blamed for AI that other parts of the business deploy.Same Thoughtworks survey · source

How Naanya closes it

Every initiative has one named owner. A monthly review reassigns or closes anything that has lost its owner.

05 · Priorities

Why do companies fund too many AI projects?

42%

of companies abandoned most of their AI initiatives in 2025, up from 17% the year before.S&P Global, as reported by CIO Dive, Mar 2025 · source

10–15%

of Johnson & Johnson's ~900 generative AI use cases produced about 80% of the value.The Batch (DeepLearning.AI), May 2025 · source

How Naanya closes it

One scorecard for every project, a monthly keep-or-close review, and a full re-score of the portfolio every quarter.

06 · Duplication

Why do companies build the same AI tool twice?

30%

of leaders at companies with 1,000+ employees say they've wasted money on redundant software. 28% use more than 10 AI apps.Zapier survey, Dec 2025 (vendor survey) · source

Few studies measure duplicate AI projects across departments. It's one of the patterns we see most often in practice.

How Naanya closes it

Every new request is checked against what already exists, and against how often the work actually repeats, before anyone builds.

07 · Adoption

Why do people stop using AI tools after launch?

51%

of frontline employees use AI regularly, and that figure has stalled. Only one in three say they've been properly trained.BCG, AI at Work 2025 · source

6%

of IT leaders piloting Microsoft 365 Copilot were planning a large-scale rollout. 3% saw significant value.Gartner survey of 132 IT leaders, as reported by Computerworld, Sep 2024 · source

How Naanya closes it

Adoption is measured, not assumed: who uses it weekly, what it replaced, and wins shared with real numbers so other teams copy them.

08 · Monitoring

What happens to AI projects after they go live?

26%

of organizations monitor their AI after it goes live, though 97% run AI in production. Two-thirds had an AI incident in the past year.BARC, Sep 2026 · source

48%

monitor production AI for accuracy, drift and misuse.Gradient Flow 2025 AI Governance Survey · source

How Naanya closes it

Every live project keeps a named owner and gets checked at day 90, then every quarter, for drift, use and value.

09 · Mid-market

What makes AI harder for mid-size companies?

92%

of middle-market firms hit challenges rolling out generative AI, though 91% use it. Among firms that weren't prepared, 39% lacked in-house expertise.RSM Middle Market AI Survey 2025, 966 respondents · source

How Naanya closes it

Large companies hire a full-time lead for this. Naanya gives mid-size companies the same system, set up in weeks and run alongside your team.

How we chose these. We used published research from analyst firms, consultancies and large surveys, and checked each figure against an accessible page. Where we could only reach news coverage of a report, the source line says so. Vendor surveys are labeled. Last checked October 2026.
