Why AI is different

Traditional software is largely complete when it ships; AI systems require continuous monitoring, evaluation, and refinement after deployment.

The hard part starts after launch. Models can drift as your data shifts, answers can degrade, a vendor update can change behavior overnight, costs can creep, adoption can fade. And when any of it happens, nothing throws an error. You find out when it has already stopped earning its cost.

Traditional launch
Build → ship → done. Keeps doing what it did on day one.
AI launch
Ship → the work begins. Accuracy can slip, a model update can shift outputs, costs can climb. And when it happens, it's silent until a customer notices.

Where the line sits: The model monitoring itself is your engineers' job, not Naanya's. Naanya makes sure it happens, that drift, quality, and value get reported, and that the findings are acted on, not filed in a plan and forgotten.
